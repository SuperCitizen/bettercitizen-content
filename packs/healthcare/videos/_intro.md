## One operation, two prices

In 2009, hospital charges for uncomplicated appendicitis in California ran from 1,529 dollars to 182,955. Every argument about American healthcare is an attempt to characterize the system that produces a number like that.

- $1,529 to $182,955

> Before judging the system, take the machine apart.

## Three lessons

The first lesson shows how the system works: where coverage comes from, where the money goes, and how the American record compares with its peers.

- 1. How U.S. healthcare works

The second gives the strongest case for each of three positions: single-payer, market reform, and expanding the Affordable Care Act. Each is presented as its smartest advocates argue it, and none is declared the winner.

- 2. The strongest cases

The third maps the policy landscape of mid-2026: a law that cuts, subsidies that expired, and the questions nobody can answer yet.

- 3. The policy landscape

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, How U.S. healthcare actually works. It begins with those two prices.
