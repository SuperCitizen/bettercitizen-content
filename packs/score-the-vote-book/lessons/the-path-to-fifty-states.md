# The Path to Fifty States

## Phase 1: Prove It (Now–2028)

Get score voting adopted in cities and counties. Build on approval voting's success in Fargo and St. Louis. Target reform-friendly jurisdictions with ballot initiative processes. Every real election builds credibility.

## Phase 2: State-Level Adoption (2028–2032)

Push for statewide score voting in states where RCV has already normalized reform. Maine and Alaska voters have already accepted that plurality voting is broken — the argument for score voting is that it's an even better solution.

## Phase 3: The Tipping Point (2032+)

When enough jurisdictions use score voting successfully, federal adoption becomes plausible. Just as RCV went from "that weird Maine thing" to a national conversation, score voting can follow the same trajectory — faster, because the reform groundwork is already laid.

## The Unified Reform Argument

The most powerful message isn't "score voting vs. RCV" — it's "anything is better than plurality, and score voting is the best alternative." Unite the reform movement first. Win against plurality. Then let the evidence determine which alternative prevails.
