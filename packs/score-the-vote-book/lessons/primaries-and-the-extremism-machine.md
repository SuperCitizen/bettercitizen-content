# Primaries and the Extremism Machine

## How Primaries Radicalize Politics

American primaries are a factory for extremism. Low turnout, closed participation, and plurality voting combine to produce candidates who represent the fringes — not the center where most voters actually live.

## Score Voting in Primaries

Score voting transforms primaries. Instead of rewarding the candidate who fires up the base while everyone else stays home, it rewards the candidate with the broadest appeal across all primary voters. Moderates and coalition-builders are no longer punished for not being extreme enough.

## The Open Primary + Score Voting Combo

The most powerful reform combination may be open primaries using score voting. Every voter scores every candidate, regardless of party. The top scorers advance to the general election. This eliminates the extremism incentive at its root.

## Alaska's Top-Four as Proof of Concept

Alaska's top-four primary (with RCV general) proved that opening up primaries changes the calculus. Now imagine that primary used score voting instead of simple plurality — the results would be even more representative.
