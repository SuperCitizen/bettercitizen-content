# Building the Movement

## Learn From RCV's Success

The RCV movement did something remarkable: it took an obscure voting theory concept and turned it into law in major American jurisdictions. Score voting advocates should study and replicate that playbook.

## Coalition, Not Partisanship

Score voting benefits everyone — left, right, and center. The most successful campaigns will be explicitly nonpartisan, focusing on fairness, simplicity, and better outcomes.

## Start Local

City council elections, school boards, mayoral races. Build a track record of score voting working in practice. Every successful local implementation makes the statewide case stronger.

## The Organizations

Groups like the Center for Election Science advocate for approval and score voting. FairVote champions RCV. The reform movement is stronger when these organizations collaborate rather than compete — even if they disagree on which system is best.

## The Messaging

Keep it simple. Keep it relatable.
- "Rate your candidates like you rate everything else"
- "Every voice counts on every candidate"  
- "The simplest fix for the biggest problem in democracy"
