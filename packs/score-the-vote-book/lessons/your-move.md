# Your Move

## What You Can Do Today

You've read the case. The math. The evidence. The real-world examples. You know that plurality voting is broken and that score voting is the most effective fix. Now what?

## Educate

Most people have never heard of score voting. Be the person who explains it. At dinner. At work. Online. The 30-second pitch: "Rate every candidate 0 to 10. Highest score wins. Like rating a movie, but for democracy."

## Advocate

Support organizations pushing for score voting and approval voting. The Center for Election Science is the leading advocacy group. Donate, volunteer, amplify.

## Vote Yes on Reform

If *any* voting reform appears on your ballot — RCV, STAR, approval, score — vote yes. The first priority is ending plurality voting. We can debate which alternative is best from a position of strength.

## Run for Office

Score voting makes it possible for anyone to run without being a spoiler. If you've ever considered running, you're exactly the kind of candidate that score voting is designed to empower.

## Don't Wait

The window for electoral reform is open. Plurality voting has survived this long not because it works, but because most people don't know there's an alternative. Now you do. Spread the word.

Democracy is not a spectator sport. Score the vote.
