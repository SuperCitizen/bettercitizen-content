# Introduction: The Ballot Is Broken

Democracy has a math problem.

You walk into a booth. You pick one name. You leave. That single mark is supposed to capture your entire political worldview — every priority, every compromise, every nuance. It can't. It never could.

The reform movement knows this. Ranked choice voting has grabbed headlines. STAR voting has its evangelists. But there's a simpler, more powerful solution hiding in plain sight — one backed by decades of social choice theory, computer simulations, and real-world use.

It's called score voting. And it's the best way to fix American elections.

This book makes that case. Not by dismissing other reforms — RCV and STAR are allies in the fight against plurality voting, and we'll give them their due. But when you compare the systems head-to-head on the metrics that actually matter — voter satisfaction, resistance to strategy, simplicity, and mathematical optimality — score voting wins. Consistently.

But this isn't just a book of arguments. It's a toolkit.

By the time you're done, you'll have the talking points to explain score voting to anyone — your skeptical uncle at Thanksgiving, your coworker who thinks voting reform sounds complicated, your friend who's given up on politics entirely. Clear, concise, impossible to dismiss.

You'll also have a path forward. If you finish this book and want to do more than nod along — if you want to actually be part of the movement — we'll show you exactly how. Where to plug in. What organizations are doing the work. How to bring score voting to your city, your county, your state.

And we'll get concrete. Not "raise awareness" platitudes — real actions. Draft ballot language. Volunteer pipelines. Donation targets. Conversations to start. Campaigns to join. This book doesn't end when you close it. It ends when your ballot changes.

Let's talk about why — and then let's talk about what you're going to do about it.
