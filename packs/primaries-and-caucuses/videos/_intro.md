## Who picks the nominee

In January 2024, New Hampshire held a presidential primary that the Democratic National Committee had declared "meaningless." The party had ordered the state to vote later. State law said first. Both had a claim to be in charge.

> Parties write the rules. States run most of the voting.

How a party picks its nominee is contested, even within each party.
