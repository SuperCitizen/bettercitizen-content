## A line on the map

Cross from one state into another, and some of the rules around you change. The money in your pocket stays the same, and so does the mail.

[beat]

That is because two governments make your rules, and each one has jobs of its own.

> Every American lives under two governments at the same time.

## Three short lessons

The first lesson asks why two friends of the same age have different rules for a driving permit.

- 1. Why two governments?

The second starts with a postage stamp. It shows which jobs belong to the national government, which belong to the states, and which they share.

- 2. Who is in charge of what?

The third tells the story of the fight over the drinking age. It shows what happens when the two governments disagree.

- 3. What happens when they disagree?

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, Why do you live under two governments? It begins with two friends who are both fourteen.

- Start with lesson 1
