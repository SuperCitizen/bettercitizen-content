## Who writes the law?

In 2025, Congress enacted 72 public laws. Federal agencies published 2,441 final rules. A final rule binds you exactly as a statute does.

- 72 public laws, 2,441 final rules

> A final rule binds you exactly as a statute does.

## Three lessons

The first lesson shows how rules actually get made: the statute that hands an agency its power, public notice and comment, White House review, and the checks that Congress and the courts hold.

- 1. How rules actually get made

The second walks the constitutional fight over that machinery, on three fronts: delegation, deference, and removal. Much of it is being decided now.

- 2. The constitutional fight

The third asks to whom the rulemakers should answer: experts, the President, or Congress. Each position gets its strongest case, and none is declared right.

- 3. The accountability debate

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, How rules actually get made. The mechanics are settled law, and every side of the argument claims to be describing them.
