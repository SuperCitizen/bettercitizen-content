## A closed room

In 1787, a group of men met in a room in Philadelphia and closed the shutters on the windows. They had been sent to fix the rules of their country. They wrote new rules, and the United States government still follows them.

> A constitution is the set of rules for a government.

## Three short lessons

The first lesson tells who wrote the Constitution, and why.

- 1. Who wrote it, and why

The second opens the Constitution up to see what it says.

- 2. What it says

The third explains how the Constitution can be changed. It ends with a college student whose idea became the Twenty-seventh Amendment.

- 3. How it can change
