# The Problem With One Vote

## Winner-Take-All Is a Losers' Game

Our current voting system — plurality voting, also called first-past-the-post — has a dirty secret: it doesn't actually find the candidate most people want. It finds the candidate who survives the most fractured opposition.

## The Spoiler Effect

Every election cycle, we hear the same argument: "Don't waste your vote on a third party." This isn't cynicism — it's rational strategy under a broken system. When voting for your actual preference can cause your least-preferred candidate to win, something is fundamentally wrong with the system, not the voters.

## Strategic Voting vs. Honest Voting

Plurality voting forces millions of Americans to lie at the ballot box every election. They don't vote *for* someone — they vote *against* someone. Ranked choice voting lets you be honest.
