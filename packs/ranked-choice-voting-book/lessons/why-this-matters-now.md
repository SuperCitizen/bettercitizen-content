# Why This Matters Now

## Democracy Under Stress

American democracy is under more stress than at any point since the Civil War. Trust in institutions is at historic lows. Polarization is at historic highs. Voter cynicism is rampant. People feel — correctly — that the system doesn't represent them.

## RCV Isn't a Silver Bullet

Ranked choice voting won't fix everything. It won't eliminate gerrymandering, dark money, or voter suppression. But it addresses something fundamental: the incentive structure of elections. When the system rewards coalition-building over base-mobilization, the entire political culture shifts.

## The Window Is Open

Reform movements have windows. The progressive era. The civil rights era. The post-Watergate reforms. Right now, there's a window for electoral reform — and ranked choice voting is the most practical, proven, and achievable reform on the table. If we don't push through it now, it may close for a generation.
