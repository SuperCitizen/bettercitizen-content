# Introduction: Your Vote Is Broken

You walk into a voting booth. You pick one person. You leave.

That's it. That's the entirety of your democratic expression — a single binary choice in a world that's anything but binary. And if you voted for the person who came in third? Your voice didn't just lose — it vanished, as if you never showed up at all.

This is how most American elections work. And it's insane.

Ranked choice voting fixes this. Not with revolution, not with constitutional amendments, not with some untested theory cooked up in a think tank. It fixes it with math, with common sense, and with a track record that's already proving itself in cities and states across the country.

This book is about that fix. Where it's been tried. How it got there. What happened when it did. And why your state should be next.
