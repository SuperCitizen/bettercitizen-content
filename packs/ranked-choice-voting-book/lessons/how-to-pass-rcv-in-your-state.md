# How to Pass RCV in Your State

## The Playbook

Every successful RCV campaign has followed a similar pattern. It starts local, builds coalitions, and frames the issue around fairness — not partisanship.

## Step 1: Build the Coalition

RCV succeeds when it's not owned by one party. The most successful campaigns have included Republicans frustrated with primaries, Democrats tired of spoilers, independents who want real choice, and good-government organizations that care about the process itself.

## Step 2: Start Local

Don't go straight for statewide implementation. Get RCV adopted in your city or county first. Build a track record. Show voters it works. Then scale up.

## Step 3: The Ballot Initiative

In states with ballot initiative processes, this is the most common path. Gather signatures, get on the ballot, and let voters decide. When voters — not legislators — make the call, RCV wins. It's passed in every statewide ballot initiative it's been on.

## Step 4: Defend It

Expect pushback. The legislature may try to repeal it (Maine). Courts may challenge it. The opposition will spread misinformation. Be ready.
