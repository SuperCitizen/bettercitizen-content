# Around the World

## Australia: A Century of Proof

Australia has used ranked choice voting (they call it "preferential voting") for federal elections since 1918. Over a century of data shows it works at scale, for a diverse population, election after election. Australia also has compulsory voting — imagine RCV with actual full participation.

## Ireland: STV and Proportional Representation

Ireland uses the single transferable vote (STV) for parliamentary elections, combining ranked choice with multi-member districts. The result is one of the most proportionally representative legislatures in the world.

## New Zealand, Scotland, and Beyond

New Zealand uses RCV for local elections. Scotland uses STV for council elections. India uses it for presidential elections. The list goes on. America isn't inventing something new — it's catching up.
