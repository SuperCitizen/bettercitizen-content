# Cities Leading the Way

## New York City

In 2019, New York City voters overwhelmingly approved RCV for primary and special elections. The 2021 mayoral primary was the first large-scale test — 13 candidates, millions of voters, and a system that worked exactly as designed. Eric Adams won after multiple rounds of tabulation, with broad coalition support.

## Minneapolis and St. Paul

Minnesota's twin cities have used RCV for local elections since 2009 and 2011, respectively. Over more than a decade, they've proven that RCV works consistently, election after election, with minimal voter confusion.

## The Growing List

Salt Lake City. San Francisco. Oakland. Arlington, Virginia. Santa Fe. Burlington (which repealed it, then brought it back). Dozens of cities across America are adopting RCV — and almost none are going back.
