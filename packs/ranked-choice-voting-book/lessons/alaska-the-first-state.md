# Alaska: The First State

## How It Happened

In 2020, Alaska voters passed Ballot Measure 2, making Alaska the first state to implement ranked choice voting for statewide elections. It wasn't a partisan effort — it was backed by an unusual coalition of independents, moderate Republicans, and reform-minded Democrats.

## The 2022 Special Election

The nation watched as Alaska held its first RCV election — a special election for the U.S. House seat left vacant by Don Young's death. Democrat Mary Peltola won, beating Republican Sarah Palin. Critics screamed it was RCV's fault. But the data told a different story: Peltola won because she was the most broadly acceptable candidate. That's the point.

## What Changed

Alaska's political culture shifted almost immediately. Candidates had to appeal beyond their base. Negative campaigning dropped. Third-party and independent candidates could run without being spoilers.
