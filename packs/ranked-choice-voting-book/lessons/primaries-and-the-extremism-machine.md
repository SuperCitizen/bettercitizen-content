# Primaries and the Extremism Machine

## How Primaries Produce Extremists

The American primary system is a factory for extremism. Low-turnout primaries, dominated by the most partisan voters, consistently produce candidates who represent the fringe — not the center.

## The General Election Hostage Situation

By the time November rolls around, voters are stuck choosing between two candidates selected by a tiny fraction of the electorate. The "moderate middle" — the actual majority — has already been locked out of the process.

## How RCV Breaks the Cycle

When voters can rank their choices in a general election with multiple candidates (as in Alaska's top-four system), the incentive to be extreme evaporates. You can't win on 30% anymore. You need broad appeal. That changes everything.
