# The Media Problem

## Why the Media Struggles With RCV

Election night coverage is built around a simple narrative: who won, who lost, how fast can we call it. Ranked choice voting disrupts this. Tabulation takes longer. Results aren't always instant. And the media — trained to report horse races — doesn't know what to do.

## Education, Not Confusion

The solution isn't to dumb down RCV to fit the media's model — it's for the media to evolve. Election coverage should explain *how* a winner was determined, not just *who* won. RCV tabulation is transparent and auditable. That's a feature, not a bug.

## The Misinformation Challenge

Social media amplifies every misunderstanding about RCV. "Your vote gets counted multiple times" (it doesn't). "It's rigged" (it's literally more transparent). "Nobody understands it" (94% of voters in RCV elections report understanding their ballot). Combating this requires consistent, simple, honest communication.
