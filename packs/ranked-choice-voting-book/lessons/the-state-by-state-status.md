# The State-by-State Status

## Where RCV Stands Today

Ranked choice voting is spreading across America faster than most people realize. Here's where things stand.

## States Using RCV

- **Alaska** — All statewide elections (since 2022)
- **Maine** — Federal elections and state primaries (since 2018)
- **Colorado** — Statewide option for local governments (since 2024)

## States With Active Campaigns

The movement is alive in dozens of states. Oregon, Nevada, Missouri, Idaho, Montana, Virginia, Massachusetts — all have active RCV campaigns at various stages.

## States That Have Banned It

Some states have preemptively banned RCV, usually through legislation pushed by party leaders who see it as a threat. Tennessee, Florida, South Dakota, and others have passed anti-RCV laws. These bans are themselves evidence that the establishment fears this reform.
