# Maine: The Pioneer

## The Governor Problem

Maine had a specific problem that made ranked choice voting almost inevitable: Paul LePage. In both 2010 and 2014, LePage won the governorship with less than 40% of the vote in three-way races. The majority of Mainers voted against him — twice — and he won anyway.

## Question 5

In 2016, Maine voters passed Question 5, adopting RCV for state primary and general elections. It was the first state to do so. The political establishment fought back hard — the legislature even tried to repeal it. Voters overrode them in a people's veto.

## Results

Maine's implementation proved that RCV works in practice, not just theory. Voter satisfaction increased. Ballot spoilage rates stayed low. And candidates started running more positive campaigns because they needed second-choice votes from their opponents' supporters.
