# Your Move

## What You Can Do Today

You've read the book. You know the facts. You understand the system. Now what?

## Vote for RCV When It's on Your Ballot

This is the easiest and most important action. When RCV appears as a ballot measure in your city, county, or state — vote yes. And convince five people you know to do the same.

## Support RCV Organizations

FairVote, the Ranked Choice Voting Resource Center, Unite America, and dozens of state-specific organizations are doing the ground-level work. They need donations, volunteers, and amplification.

## Talk About It

The biggest barrier to RCV isn't opposition — it's obscurity. Most Americans don't know what ranked choice voting is. Be the person who explains it at dinner, at work, on social media. Make it part of the conversation.

## Run for Office

Seriously. RCV makes it possible for regular people to run and win. You don't need a party machine behind you. You need good ideas and broad appeal. If you've ever thought about running, RCV might be the system that makes it viable.

## Don't Wait for Permission

Democracy is not a spectator sport. The system won't fix itself. The people who benefit from the broken system will never voluntarily change it. That's on us. That's on you. Start now.
