# The Path to Fifty States

## A Roadmap for National Adoption

Getting all fifty states to adopt ranked choice voting won't happen overnight. But it can happen — and the momentum is already building.

## Phase 1: The Early Adopters (Now–2028)

The first phase is already underway. Maine and Alaska have proven the model. Colorado has opened the door for local adoption. The next targets are states with ballot initiative processes and reform-minded electorates: Oregon, Nevada, Montana, Idaho, and others.

## Phase 2: The Tipping Point (2028–2032)

Once 10–15 states have adopted RCV, the national conversation changes. It goes from "that weird thing Alaska does" to "the standard in modern American elections." Federal candidates from RCV states will advocate for it nationally.

## Phase 3: The New Normal (2032+)

The endgame is federal legislation — or, more likely, enough state adoption that plurality voting becomes the relic it deserves to be. When the majority of Americans vote with ranked ballots, the holdout states will face increasing pressure from their own voters.
