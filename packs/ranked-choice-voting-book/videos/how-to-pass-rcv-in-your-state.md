## A campaign you win three times

This is a chapter of Rank the Vote. It is an argument, and the "I" in it is the book's author.

On Election Day 2014, volunteers in Maine stood outside polling places and collected about 40,000 signatures in a single day. In November 2016, Maine voted 52 to 48 to adopt ranked-choice voting. Then the legislature tried to delay and repeal it, and in June 2018 those same volunteers won again, 54 to 46.

> It is a campaign you win three times: adoption, implementation, and defense.

## Which door your state has

Your state constitution has already decided how you get in. Twenty-six states, plus D.C., give citizens the ballot initiative, the popular referendum, or both. Maine and Alaska, the only two states using ranked ballots statewide, both got there by initiative.

- 26 states plus D.C.: initiative or referendum

Everywhere else, the path runs through the legislature. It is slower, and it arrives in smaller packages. Hawaii's legislature adopted ranked ballots in 2022 for federal special elections and county-council vacancies, where no sitting legislator's seat is at stake.

- Legislature: slower, smaller first steps

## What 2024 taught

I will not spin this. November 2024 was the worst night the statewide movement has had. Adoption measures lost in Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, Oregon and Arizona. Nevada's measure had passed its first vote in 2022. It failed its required second vote with 47 percent.

- 2024: five statewide measures lost

I draw three lessons. Bundling cuts both ways. The opposition showed up funded and organized. And the third is the oldest.

> Status-quo bias is the house edge: a confused voter votes no.

## After the yes vote

A yes vote buys you an engineering project: equipment, voter education, and the fine print. Utah is the cautionary tale. Its 2018 pilot let cities opt in, and 23 did in 2021. But pilots expire. On January 1, 2026, the program sunset, and every participating city reverted to plurality elections. Nobody repealed it. It was allowed to lapse.

- Utah pilot: 23 cities in 2021
- Sunset: January 1, 2026

> A sunset clause is a reform with a pre-installed self-destruct.
