## More votes, fewer seats

In 1812 a Massachusetts governor, Elbridge Gerry, signed a map with a district so strange that a newspaper drew it as a monster. That spring his opponents won more votes in the state's senate races. His party took the senate, 29 seats to 11.

> More votes, fewer seats: that is what the word names.

The practice is older than its name, and very much alive in the 2026 midterms.
