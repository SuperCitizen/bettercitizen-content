## Not "Republican lite"

Libertarianism is often filed under conservative economics. This pack treats it as a distinct tradition, with strong commitments that often align with neither conservatism nor progressivism.

> A distinct tradition, often aligned with neither conservatism nor progressivism.

## Three lessons

The first lesson sets out what the tradition actually believes, as its adherents would put it. It opens with a basketball star and a box of quarters.

- 1. What libertarianism believes

The second covers the disagreements inside the family: minarchists, anarcho-capitalists and bleeding-heart libertarians. Each stream makes its best case, and none is declared the winner.

- 2. Internal disagreements

The third turns outward, to how libertarianism relates to conservatism, progressivism and socialism, and to what its ideas have changed in American law.

- 3. Across the spectrum

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, What libertarianism actually believes.
