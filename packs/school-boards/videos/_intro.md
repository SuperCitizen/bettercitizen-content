## A government few can name

There are 13,318 school districts in America, and roughly 90,000 people sit on their boards. Millions of residents could not name a single member of their own.

- 13,318 districts
- Roughly 90,000 board members

Those boards adopt the budget, hire the superintendent, and decide which schools open and close.

> The school board is the most reachable government you have.

## Two lessons

The first lesson shows the machine: what a board controls, what the state and the federal government control, where the money comes from, and who votes for all of it.

- 1. How school boards work

The second is the user's manual: finding your district, reading the agenda before the meeting, and taking each concern to the right door.

- 2. How to engage

Neither lesson takes a side in the fights over phones, books or curricula. The subject here is the institution.
