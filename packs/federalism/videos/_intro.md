## Two sovereigns, one storefront

Buy marijuana at a licensed dispensary in Denver, and you have followed state law and committed a federal crime. Both governments are acting within their lawful authority.

> Federalism is a permanent, structured argument over who governs what.

## Three lessons

The first lesson builds the architecture: which powers are federal, which belong to the states, and which both share.

- 1. Federal vs. state: who can do what?

The second follows the money: grants, the strings attached to them, and the point where the Supreme Court said a string becomes a leash.

- 2. The price of federalism

The third walks the open conflicts: preemption, marijuana, sanctuary jurisdictions, and the state attorneys general.

- 3. Federal–state conflict today

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, Federal versus state: who can do what? It begins in that dispensary.
