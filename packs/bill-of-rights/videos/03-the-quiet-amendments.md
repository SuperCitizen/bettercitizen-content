## Soldiers in the apartments

In the spring of 1979, New York's correction officers went on strike, and the governor called out the National Guard to run the prisons. At one prison, officials evicted striking officers from their rented staff housing and moved guardsmen in.

Two officers sued under the Third Amendment, ratified in 1791 to stop soldiers from being quartered in private homes. Their case, Engblom v. Carey, remains the only significant Third Amendment decision in American history.

- Engblom v. Carey, 1982

> Four amendments almost never appear in court, each for a different reason.

## Quiet because it won

The Third Amendment answered Britain's Quartering Acts, which forced colonists to house troops. Then the problem disappeared. A professional military housed on bases never needs your spare bedroom.

- Third: the problem disappeared

The Seventh preserves the jury in federal civil suits. It binds only federal courts, and civil trials themselves have nearly vanished. By 2002, under 2 percent of federal civil cases were resolved by trial.

- Seventh: federal courts only
- Civil trials: under 2% by 2002

## Rights and powers

The Ninth and Tenth are constantly swapped. The Ninth is about rights: listing certain rights shall not be construed to deny others retained by the people. The Tenth is about powers: those not delegated to the United States are reserved to the states or the people.

- Ninth: rights the people retain
- Tenth: powers never delegated

> The Ninth is about rights. The Tenth is about powers.

## The truism that woke up

In 1941 the Supreme Court called the Tenth "but a truism." For most of half a century, that held. Then the Court found a real rule inside it, the anti-commandeering doctrine. Congress may not conscript the states' own governments as its instruments.

- 1941: "but a truism"

In Printz, in 1997, Congress could not order local sheriffs to run handgun background checks. In Murphy, in 2018, it could not forbid states from legalizing sports betting.

- Printz, 1997: no conscripting state officers
- Murphy, 2018: sports betting

> Congress may encourage, fund and preempt, but not dragoon.

[check q4-commandeering-transfer]

## Silence has causes

The Ninth is quiet because nobody agrees what it does. Randy Barnett reads it as protecting natural rights beyond the list. Kurt Lash reads it as guarding state self-government. The lesson does not resolve that.

- Ninth: its meaning is contested
