## An unlikely compliment

In 2024, Senator J.D. Vance was months away from becoming the Republican vice-presidential nominee. He called Lina Khan, the Biden FTC chair championed by progressive antitrust reformers, one of the few people in that administration doing a pretty good job.

- Vance on Khan, 2024

Partisan maps cannot explain that. Ideological maps can.

> Progressivism's borders with neighboring traditions are blurry.

## With conservatism: change

Progressives and conservatives share a surprising amount. Both worry about concentrated private power. New Deal progressives and national conservatives both center the working class.

- Shared: working class, concentrated power

What divides them is inherited institutions. Progressives emphasize changing them toward more inclusive and equal forms. Conservatives emphasize conserving them against destabilizing change.

- Divide: change or conserve

## With libertarianism: markets

Progressives and libertarians substantially agree on civil liberties: speech protection, surveillance limits, drug-policy and criminal-justice reform. They divide on economic policy. Libertarians treat market exchange as the legitimate ordering principle. Progressives treat market outcomes as politically constructed, and politically correctable.

- Shared: civil liberties
- Divide: markets

## With socialism: how far

Progressives and democratic socialists share commitments to universal healthcare, strong labor rights and a critique of corporate power. Progressives generally accept market capitalism, with strong regulation and social insurance. Democratic socialists generally seek to transcend it, through worker ownership for example.

- Progressives: regulate and insure the market
- Democratic socialists: transcend it

> Know which argument you are in: change, markets, or how far correction goes.

## Where partisan lines mislead

On antitrust, industrial policy and family policy, the lesson finds support that crosses the parties, even when the political vehicles fail.

- Convergence: antitrust, industrial policy, family policy

The lesson adds a fourth neighbor, communitarianism, sets American progressives beside European social democrats, and lays the four borders side by side. It leaves open whether today's convergences are durable realignments or marriages of convenience.
