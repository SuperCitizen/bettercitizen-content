## What you think you know

Almost everyone has an opinion about taxes. Far fewer have been shown how the federal system works: where the money comes from, who pays it, and what is taxed.

> How much, who pays, and what is taxed: three separate questions.

## Three lessons

The first lesson shows how federal taxes actually work, from the brackets to the taxes on a pay stub.

- 1. How federal taxes work

The second gives the strongest case for each of three ways to change them: progressive expansion, supply-side reform, and a consumption tax. Each gets its best argument, and none is declared the winner.

- 2. The strongest cases

The third covers what is being fought over now, and what the evidence can and cannot settle.

- 3. The policy landscape

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, How U.S. federal taxes actually work. It begins with a raise that somebody refused.
