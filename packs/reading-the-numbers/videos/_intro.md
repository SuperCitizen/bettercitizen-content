## The biggest sample ever collected

In 1936 a magazine collected 2.4 million ballots and missed the presidential election by 19 points. A random sample of about 1,000 people can read a whole country within about 3 points.

- 2.4 million ballots: missed by 19
- 1,000 people: about ±3 points

The difference is not size. It is how the number was made.

> The procedure decides what a number can mean.

## Three lessons

The first lesson covers polls: what a margin of error covers, the kinds of error it leaves out, and the record of recent elections.

- 1. How polls work, and fail

The second covers studies: the ladder from anecdote to randomized experiment, and what it takes to earn the word "because".

- 2. Correlation, causation, the study

The third covers forecasts and official statistics: what a probability claims, and when it deserves trust.

- 3. Models, forecasts, what to trust

Where the methods are disputed, each lesson gives the competing positions their case.
