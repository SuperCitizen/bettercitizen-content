## The government nobody watches

A city council votes on the budget, the rezoning and the police contract. No other level of American government is as easy to reach, and almost nobody reaches for it.

> The government nearest to your daily life is the one fewest people watch.

## Two lessons

The first lesson shows how a city council works: the forms a city can choose, how members are elected, how an ordinance becomes law, and how much the state can take back.

- 1. How city councils work

The second is practical. It shows which door to knock on, when, and with what in hand, from a 311 request to a committee hearing to three minutes of public comment.

- 2. How to engage with yours

The pack notes the disputes over how councils should be built and elected. It does not settle them.

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, How U.S. city councils actually work. It begins in Portland, Oregon, which changed its whole form of government in January 2025.
