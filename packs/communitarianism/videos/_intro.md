## The tradition with no party

Two thinkers lodged the same complaint against modern liberalism in the early 1980s. One became a foundation for conservative religious thought, the other for the communitarian left. Communitarianism has serious adherents among progressives, conservatives and religious thinkers.

> One philosophical core, with adherents on the left and the right.

## Three lessons

The first lesson sets out what the tradition believes, in terms its adherents would accept.

- 1. What it believes

The second follows its three streams, civic-republican, religious-traditionalist and progressive, to the places where they break from one another. Each gets its own case, and none is declared right.

- 2. Internal disagreements

The third shows how communitarianism relates to conservatism, progressivism, socialism and libertarianism.

- 3. Across the spectrum
