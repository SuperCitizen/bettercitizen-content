## Where the government has to stop

The Constitution says a great deal about what the government can do. The Bill of Rights says what the government may not do to you. It is the first ten amendments.

> The Bill of Rights is a list of limits on the government.

## Three lessons, three stories

Each lesson is built around real people.

The first tells why a list of rights was added at all. It follows George Mason, the delegate who would not sign the Constitution.

- 1. Why a Bill of Rights?

The second covers the First Amendment and its five freedoms. It follows students in Iowa who wore black armbands to school.

- 2. The First Amendment

The third goes through the other nine amendments. It follows Clarence Gideon, a prisoner who wrote to the Supreme Court by hand.

- 3. The other nine

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, Why does the Constitution have a Bill of Rights? It begins on the day one man would not pick up the pen.

- Start with lesson 1
