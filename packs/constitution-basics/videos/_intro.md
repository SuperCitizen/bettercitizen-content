## About 4,400 words

The Constitution runs about 4,400 words, and it is the oldest written national constitution still in operation. Almost everything Americans argue about in politics eventually routes back to some clause of it.

> A short text, a stiff way to change it, a thick layer of convention.

## Three lessons

The first lesson maps the Preamble and the seven Articles: what the Constitution actually says, and what it set out to do.

- 1. What's actually in the Constitution?

The second takes the Article Five amendment machine apart, from the Twenty-seventh Amendment's 202-year ratification to the Equal Rights Amendment's contested present.

- 2. Amending the Constitution

The third covers the unwritten constitution of precedent, practice and norm that does much of the daily governing.

- 3. The unwritten constitution
