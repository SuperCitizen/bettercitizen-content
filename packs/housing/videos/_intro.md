## The first home, at 40

The median first-time homebuyer in the United States is now 40 years old. Through the 1980s, the typical first-timer was in their late twenties. Politicians of every stripe have noticed. They do not agree on why, or on what to do.

> The median first-time homebuyer is now 40 years old.

## Three lessons

The first lesson shows the machine itself: who decides whether homes get built, who finances them, and who gets help paying for them.

The second gives the strongest case for each of three approaches: supply-side zoning reform, demand-side subsidies and assistance, and tenant protection. Each is built the way its smartest advocate would build it, and none is declared the winner.

- 2. The strongest cases

The third maps the field as it stands in mid-2026: states overriding local zoning, federal housing money, and the response to homelessness.
