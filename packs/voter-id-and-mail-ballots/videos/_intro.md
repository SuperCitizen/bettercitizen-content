## Five minutes apart

On one side of the Snake River, Oregonians get their ballots in the mail automatically. On the other, Idahoans mostly vote in person, after showing a photo ID. Five minutes apart, two complete answers to the same question.

> America has no single way to vote. It has fifty-one systems.
