## Fewer than two in a hundred

In 2023 and 2024, members of Congress introduced 16,202 bills. Only 274 measures became law. And yet some very large laws got through anyway.

- 16,202 bills, 274 laws

> Most bills die. The big ones find another way through.

## Two lessons

The first lesson asks why most bills never become law. It separates the steps the Constitution requires from the hurdles each chamber added through its own rules.

- 1. Why most bills die

The second asks how big laws get passed anyway. It follows the routes around the roadblock, with real votes under both parties.

- 2. How big laws pass

Both lessons are built on Article One of the Constitution and on Hamilton's Federalist Number 73.
