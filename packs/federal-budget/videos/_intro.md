## Seven trillion dollars

Asked what share of the federal budget goes to foreign aid, Americans on average answer about a quarter. It is roughly one percent. The federal government spends 7 trillion dollars a year, and its interest bill now exceeds defense.

> Where seven trillion dollars actually goes.

## Three lessons

The first lesson is the map: mandatory programs, discretionary spending, and interest.

- 1. Where the money actually goes

The second gives the strongest case for each of three readings of the deficit: the fiscal consolidation hawk, the full-employment dove, and the public-investment advocate.

- 2. The strongest cases

The third shows how the process works, and how it breaks: appropriations, shutdowns, reconciliation, the debt ceiling, and the impoundment fights of 2025 and 2026.

- 3. How the process actually works

## Where to start

The composition of spending is settled arithmetic. What the deficit means, and who should bear the adjustment, is contested. Start with the first lesson, Where the money actually goes. It opens with a guess.
