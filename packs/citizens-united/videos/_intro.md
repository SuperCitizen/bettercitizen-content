## What it did not hold

Citizens United is often summarized as the case that ruled corporations are people. That is not what it held. In 2010 the Supreme Court held that the government may not ban political speech because the speaker is a corporation or a union.

> Government may not ban political speech because the speaker is a corporation or union.

Whether that was right is contested, and this pack does not settle it.
