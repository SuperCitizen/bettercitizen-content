## What it protects, and against whom

"You can't shout fire in a crowded theater" is the most quoted line in American free-speech argument. It is not the law, and has not been for more than half a century.

The actual doctrine is among the most speech-protective in the world, and nearly every piece of it is contested by serious people on serious grounds.

> The First Amendment restrains governments, not private actors.

## Three lessons

The first lesson shows whom the First Amendment binds, and the short list of unprotected categories of speech, each with its test.
