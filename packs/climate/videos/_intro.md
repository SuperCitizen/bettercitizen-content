## Two questions

Climate debate routinely confuses two different questions. Is the warming real and human-caused? And what should be done about it? The first is settled science. The second is genuinely contested policy.

> The science is settled in its core claims. What to do is not.

## Three lessons

The first lesson sets out what the science establishes, and what it cannot decide.

- 1. The science and the stakes

The second gives the strongest case for each of three approaches: aggressive mitigation, adaptation first, and innovation led. Each gets its best argument, and none is declared the winner.

- 2. The strongest cases

The third maps the policy landscape: carbon pricing, regulation and subsidy, the Inflation Reduction Act and its partial repeal, the courts, the states, and the second United States exit from Paris.

- 3. The policy landscape

## Where to start

Start with the first lesson, The science and the stakes. It begins in 1958, on a bare lava field in Hawaii, with one instrument and one reading.
