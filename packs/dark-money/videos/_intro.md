## Who paid for that ad

A political ad closes with the words "Paid for by Americans for a Better Tomorrow." Every filing behind it can be accurate, and the person whose money it was can still be nowhere in the record.

> Dark money: election spending whose original source is concealed.

Some people see a scandal in that. Others see a shield. This pack gives each view its case.

## Three lessons

The first lesson shows how the money actually flows, hop by hop, and how much of it there is.

- 1. How dark money flows

The second gives the strongest case for each of three positions: the disclosure-first reformer, the donor-privacy advocate, and the structural-reform centrist. None is declared the winner.

- 2. The strongest case for and against

The third maps the policy landscape as of mid-2026: a bill that keeps failing in the Senate, regulators who cannot act, and the states that are writing their own rules.

- 3. The policy landscape
