## 3.4 percent

Between 2014 and 2018, the twenty-five richest Americans grew about 401 billion dollars richer. Over those same five years they paid 13.6 billion dollars in federal income tax. ProPublica divided one number by the other and called the result a true tax rate: 3.4 percent. Almost nothing in the files was illegal.

- Wealth growth: about $401 billion
- Federal income tax: $13.6 billion

> Is 3.4 percent an outrage, or just arithmetic?

## Buy, borrow, die

The keystone has three steps. Buy appreciating assets and hold them. The gain is not income until you sell. Borrow against them to fund your life. A loan is not income at all. Then die holding them.

- Buy: an unsold gain is not income
- Borrow: a loan is not income

Section 1014 of the tax code says that when an asset is inherited, its basis resets to fair-market value at the owner's death. Basis is the figure a taxable gain is measured from. This is the stepped-up basis. It erases a lifetime of unrealized gain, so the appreciation is never income-taxed by anyone.

- Die: the basis resets, the gain is erased

> The tax is not so much evaded as designed around.

## Avoidance, not evasion

Tax avoidance is arranging your affairs to owe less, lawfully. Tax evasion is illegally failing to pay what is owed. Buy, borrow, die is avoidance. So the serious claim about the United States is plutocracy, power that derives from wealth and operates lawfully. It is not kleptocracy, which is defined by the crime.

- Avoidance: lawful
- Evasion: illegal

> The question is about the statute, not the taxpayer.

## The denominator

The 3.4 percent measures tax against wealth growth, mostly unrealized gains, which the United States has never treated as income. Critics answer that this is not the rate anyone's tax return computes. Defenders of the framing reply that this is the point: the conventional measures miss the gains entirely.

- Critics: no tax return computes this rate
- Defenders: the usual measures miss the gains

The choice of denominator is where this argument is fought.
