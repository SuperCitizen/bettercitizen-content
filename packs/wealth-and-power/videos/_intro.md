## Almost nothing was illegal

Between 2014 and 2018, the twenty-five richest Americans grew about 401 billion dollars richer, and paid 13.6 billion dollars in federal income tax. Almost nothing in the files behind those numbers was illegal. So the question is not whether a crime was committed.

> Does concentrated wealth rule American politics?

## Three lessons

The first lesson is the machinery: how large fortunes are legally defended against taxation, and how concentrated wealth actually is.

- 1. The wealth-defense machine

The second gives the strongest case for each of three camps on whether that wealth captures policy: the oligarchy realist, the pluralist skeptic and the public-choice view.

- 2. Does wealth actually rule?

The third maps the fights of 2025 and 2026, from wealth taxes and Moore against United States to the global minimum tax and antitrust.
