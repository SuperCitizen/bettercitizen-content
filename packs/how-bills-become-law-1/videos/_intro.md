## A walk and a law

In 2016, Opal Lee set out to walk from Texas toward Washington, D.C. She was 89 years old, and she wanted Juneteenth to be a holiday for the whole country. Five years later, it was one. But a walk cannot make a law.

> Only Congress can make a law. So how does an idea get there?

## Three short lessons

The first lesson shows how an idea turns into a bill, which is a written plan for a new law.

The second follows a bill inside Congress, where most bills stop for good.
