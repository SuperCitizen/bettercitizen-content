## Two numbers

Federal law says no person may give a political action committee more than 5,000 dollars a year. In the 2024 election cycle, one donor put more than 291 million dollars into electing his preferred candidates. He broke no law.

- The limit: $5,000 a year
- One donor, 2024: more than $291 million

> A PAC and a super PAC are different machines, under different rules.
