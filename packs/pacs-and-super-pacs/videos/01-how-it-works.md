## Two true sentences

In the 2024 election cycle, Elon Musk put more than 291 million dollars into electing his preferred candidates. Federal law says that no person may give a political action committee more than 5,000 dollars a year.

- Musk, 2024: more than $291 million
- PAC limit: $5,000 a year

[beat]

> Both sentences are true, and nobody broke the law.

## Two doors

There are two doors into a federal race. The first is the traditional PAC. A donor may give it 5,000 dollars a year, and it may give a candidate up to 5,000 dollars per election.

- Traditional PAC: capped in, capped out

The second door opened in 2010. In January, the Supreme Court held in Citizens United that independent spending, meaning spending not coordinated with a candidate, does not corrupt. Two months later, an appeals court drew the next step. If independent spending cannot corrupt, then giving to a group that only spends independently cannot corrupt either, and the 5,000 dollar cap on it falls.

That group is the super PAC. It may take unlimited money from individuals, corporations and unions. The price is that it gives nothing to candidates or parties, and does not coordinate with them.

> A super PAC: unlimited money in, nothing to candidates, no coordination.

[check q1]

## Who can see the donors

Here is the fact most often mangled. Super PACs are not secret. The same court upheld their reporting requirements, and a super PAC itemizes its donors to the FEC.

- Super PAC donors: public FEC reports

What the report cannot tell you is who stands behind a donor that is itself a nondisclosing nonprofit or a shell company. In 2024, super PACs and similar committees reported receiving over 1 billion dollars from such sources.

> Super PACs name their donors. The trail can still dead-end one step back.

## Every cap reroutes

The PAC itself began as a workaround, built in 1943 after Congress barred union treasury contributions. Two scholars, La Raja and Schaffner, add a caution: squeezing the regulated channels redirected money into outside groups. Every cap reroutes, and nothing evaporates.

- Every cap reroutes

Whether this architecture is required free speech, plutocratic capture, or a fixable enforcement failure is disputed. The lesson has the century of bans and workarounds, the hybrid PAC with its two bank accounts, the leadership PAC, and the verified scale of 2024. The next lesson builds each of the three cases.
