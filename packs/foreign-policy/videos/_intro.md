## An invitation to struggle

The Constitution gives Congress the power to declare war. Congress last used it in June 1942, and American forces have fought many times since. Edward Corwin called the Constitution "an invitation to struggle for the privilege of directing American foreign policy."

> The question is what America's enormous power is for.

## Three lessons

The first lesson maps the machine: who holds which lever, from war powers and treaties to alliances, sanctions and aid.

- 1. How foreign policy actually works

The second gives the strongest case for each of three schools: liberal internationalism, restraint, and conservative realism. Each is heard as its smartest advocate would put it, and none is declared the winner.

- 2. The strongest cases

The third takes those schools to the live debates over China, Russia and Ukraine, the Middle East, and trade.

- 3. The current policy landscape
