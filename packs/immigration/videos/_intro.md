## Opposite answers from the same data

Two economists studied the same city, the same sudden arrival of immigrants, and the same public data. One found no effect on wages. The other found a steep fall.

Immigration is contested twice over: by values, and by evidence.

> The empirical disagreements are sharper than the political rhetoric admits.

## Three lessons

The first lesson shows how immigration law actually works: the visa system, asylum, the unauthorized population, and who controls which lever.

The second gives the strongest case for each of three positions: restrictionist, expansionist, and comprehensive-reform centrist. Each gets its best argument, and none is declared the winner.
