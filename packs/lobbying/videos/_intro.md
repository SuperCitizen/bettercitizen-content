## Protected, and enormous

Lobbying is protected by the First Amendment's petition clause. It is also a business that disclosed more than 5 billion dollars of spending in 2025, and the disclosed part is a floor.

- Protected by the petition clause
- Over $5 billion disclosed in 2025

> The trade is older than every statute that regulates it.
