## Just a bill

In the old Schoolhouse Rock cartoon, a bill sits on the Capitol steps, and by the end of the song he is a law. In the 118th Congress, members introduced more than 19,000 bills and resolutions. Exactly 274 became public law.

- Introduced: more than 19,000
- Enacted: 274

> Fewer than two in every hundred became law.

## Three lessons

The first lesson walks the official path, from introduction to the President's desk, and the five points where a bill can be killed.

- 1. From idea to enacted law

The second shows how major laws actually pass now: omnibus packages, continuing resolutions and reconciliation.

- 2. How laws really get made

The third is the arithmetic that explains where most bills die: 218, 51, 60, 67.

- 3. Why bills die
