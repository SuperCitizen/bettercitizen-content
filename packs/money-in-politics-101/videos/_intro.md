## 6,600 dollars, or 290 million

In 2024, one donor could legally hand a presidential campaign 6,600 dollars, and legally put more than 290 million dollars into the election. Both are true, and both are legal.

> Most money arguments fail because people are not talking about the same channel.

## Three lessons

The first lesson maps the five channels a political dollar can flow through: which are capped, which are disclosed, and which must stay independent of the candidate.

- 1. The flow of political money

The second covers the law that drew those lines, from a two-sentence ban in 1907 to Citizens United and the cases after it.

- 2. The legal framework

The third turns practical: how to look before you give. It will not tell you whom to support.

- 3. Giving thoughtfully
